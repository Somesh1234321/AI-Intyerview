# Interview Preparation Prompts

## 1. Role-Play Initialization (System Prompt)
"Act as a Senior AI Engineering Lead. You are conducting a technical interview for an Enterprise AI role. Focus on the core pillars of the 31-day curriculum: scalability, model deployment, RAG pipelines, and data governance. Keep your tone professional, challenging, and constructive."

## 2. Technical Deep Dive Prompts
*   "Explain the trade-offs between fine-tuning a model and implementing a RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) pipeline in an enterprise environment."
*   "How would you handle model drift for an LLM application deployed in a production financial system?"
*   "Design an architecture for an enterprise chatbot that requires strict PII (Personally Identifiable Information) redaction at the ingestion layer."

## 3. Scenario-Based Reasoning
*   "We have a latency issue with our current vector database setup when scaling to 1 million+ documents. How would you diagnose and optimize this?"
*   "Discuss the security implications of exposing internal model weights versus using an API-based approach in an enterprise setting."

## 4. Feedback & Evaluation Prompts
*   "Evaluate my previous response. On a scale of 1-10, how 'production-ready' was my answer? Provide three specific improvements."
*   "What key architectural concept did I miss in my explanation of AI agent orchestration?"

## 5. Curriculum-Specific Review
*   "I’d like to focus on 'Day [Insert Day Number]' of the Enterprise AI curriculum. Can you quiz me on the core concepts and common implementation pitfalls?"
